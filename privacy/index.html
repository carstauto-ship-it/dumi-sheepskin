Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Jinan Carstauto Accessories Co., Limited ("we", "us", "our") operates dumi-sheepskin.com and lambskin.com.cn (together, the "Sites") and manufactures natural sheepskin products under the DUMI and DODOMI brands — car seat covers, floor mats, rugs, cushions, wash mitts and medical sheepskin. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit a quote request on the Sites or complete one of our advertising lead forms, we collect the details you enter — typically your name, email address, country or region, telephone or messaging number (optional), company name, business type, the product you are interested in, estimated order quantity and any message you send us.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit the Sites, our hosting provider and our analytics or advertising tools may record technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referring links.
  • Advertising and measurement data. We use advertising and measurement services provided by Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram). These services may use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to record that you visited our Sites or interacted with our advertising, and to measure the performance of our campaigns.

We do not intentionally collect information from anyone under 18, and we do not collect payment card details through the Sites.

2. How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare quotations, samples and shipping terms;
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry by email, telephone or messaging apps;
  • To process and fulfil orders where you place one;
  • To measure and improve the performance of our websites and our advertising;
  • To comply with legal, accounting and export obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your enquiry details with other buyers or with unrelated third parties.

3. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate our business, and only as far as necessary:

  • Meta Platforms — for advertising delivery and campaign measurement, including the hosting of lead forms;
  • Hosting, email and communication service providers — to operate the Sites and to send and receive messages;
  • Logistics and payment partners — where required to ship an order you place;
  • Professional advisers and competent authorities — where required by law.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The Sites and our advertising may use cookies, pixels and local storage to keep the Sites working, to remember your language preference, and to measure advertising performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the Sites may then not work as intended. You can also adjust your advertising preferences in your Meta account settings.

5. International transfers

We are based in China and we sell internationally. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including China and the countries in which our service providers operate. Where required, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

6. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and quotation records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, accounting and export requirements, and we delete or anonymise them when they are no longer needed. Advertising measurement data is retained according to the retention periods set by the relevant platform.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent you previously gave. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 8. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.
