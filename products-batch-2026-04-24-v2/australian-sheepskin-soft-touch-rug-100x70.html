Discover the signature softness that Australian merino sheepskin is celebrated for worldwide with our Australian Sheepskin Soft Touch Rug, sized at 100x70cm. This premium rug undergoes a special soft-touch finishing process that enhances the already-remarkable natural softness of merino wool, creating a surface so gentle it feels like a gentle caress under bare feet. The 100x70cm dimensions make this rug perfectly suited for placement beside beds where you want a warm landing spot upon waking, in nurseries for safe baby playtime, or as an accent piece in living rooms where its touch-me texture invites interaction. Australian merino wool is finer than standard sheep's wool, with fibers measuring just 15-24 microns in diameter compared to coarser wools that can reach 40+ microns. This fineness is what creates the almost silk-like feel that makes merino sheepskin the world's most sought-after variety. Beyond its luxurious texture, this soft touch rug offers all the natural benefits of genuine sheepskin: temperature regulation, moisture management, and inherent fire resistance.
